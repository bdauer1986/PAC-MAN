Privacy Policy

This application accesses Intuit QuickBooks Online data solely to perform
accounting and financial management functions for authorized users.

Information accessed may include:

- Account records
- Transactions
- Vendor and customer information
- Financial reports

This data is used only for internal business operations and is not sold,
shared, or distributed to third parties.

Security:
Access to QuickBooks data is authenticated through Intuit's OAuth
authorization system.

Data Storage:
Data retrieved from QuickBooks may be temporarily stored for processing
within secure internal systems.
